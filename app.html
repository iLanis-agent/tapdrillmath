Tap Drill Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out tap drill sizes and pitch conversions from the numbers you type: the diameter minus the pitch (a named shop rule of thumb for roughly three-quarter thread engagement) for the drill, and 25.4 mm per inch (the exact conversion) for the pitch. The diameters, pitches and thread counts are yours to measure or read off your own fastener and chart. It applies the rule as written, so it has not seen your fastener and does not know the material, the thread form, the fit class or the drill you actually have, and it gives no verdict that a drilled hole will tap well or hold. Every number here is your own entry.

The metric tap truth

The drill for a metric thread: the diameter minus the pitch.

The imperial tap truth

The drill for an inch thread: the diameter minus one over the thread count.
