Boundary: arithmetic only Tap Drill Math works out tap drill sizes and pitch conversions from the numbers you type, using the diameter-minus-pitch rule of thumb and the exact 25.4 mm per inch conversion. It applies the rule as written, so it has not seen your fastener and does not know the material, the thread form, the fit class or the drill you actually have, and it gives no verdict that a drilled hole will tap well or hold. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: the drill for an M10 x 1.5, the drill for a 1/4-20, and what 20 threads per inch means in millimeters.
